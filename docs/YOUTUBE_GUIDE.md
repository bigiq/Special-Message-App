# How to Set Up Your Tracking Video 🎥

The "Special Message App" uses a clever way to let you know when your recipient has seen your message. By using a personal YouTube video, you can track the "view count" as a notification.

## Setup and Configuration Process

The initial step in establishing your notification system is the creation of a **YouTube Channel**. You may accomplish this by signing into the YouTube platform and selecting the option to create a channel from your profile menu. Once the channel is active, you should prepare a personalized video message. This content can be a direct recording or an AI-generated animation, provided it serves as a meaningful conclusion to your interactive message.

When uploading your video, it is critical to navigate to the **Visibility** settings and select the **Unlisted** option. An unlisted status ensures that your video remains private from the general public and search results, accessible only to those who possess the direct URL. After the upload is complete, copy the provided video link for use in the application builder.

To finalize the integration, open the Special Message Builder and navigate to the **seventh option** field. Paste your copied URL into the designated section for the personal video link. Once your recipient opens the generated HTML file and interacts with the final option, you can monitor the **view count** on your YouTube Studio dashboard. An increase in views will serve as a definitive confirmation that your special message has been successfully received and viewed.
